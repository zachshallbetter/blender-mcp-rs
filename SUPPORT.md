# Support

`blender-mcp-rs` is maintained by one person. Support is best-effort, with no SLA.

- **Questions and bugs:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/zachshallbetter/blender-mcp-rs/issues/new/choose). For a bug, include the `blender-mcp` version, the Blender version and your operating system.
- **Security reports:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
- **Versioning:** releases follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org) under [VERSIONING.md](VERSIONING.md), tagged `vX.Y.Z`. The project is pre-1.0.
